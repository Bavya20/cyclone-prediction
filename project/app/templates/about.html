About Our Research

We're pioneering the future of cyclone forecasting through advanced spatiotemporal modeling and machine learning. Our mission is to save lives and reduce economic impact through more accurate predictions.

Our Mission

To revolutionize cyclone track forecasting by developing cutting-edge spatiotemporal models that significantly improve prediction accuracy, enabling better disaster preparedness and response strategies worldwide. We aim to reduce the human and economic toll of cyclones through timely, precise forecasts.

Our Vision

To create a world where no community is caught unprepared for severe weather events. We envision a global forecasting system that leverages artificial intelligence, real-time satellite data, and collaborative research to provide accurate, accessible predictions for all vulnerable regions.
